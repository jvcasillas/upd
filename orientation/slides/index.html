Placement

Placement (SAS Policy in Global Languages)

 

All students (heritage or non-heritage) who wish to enroll in classes in Spanish and Portuguese must take a placement exam IF they have taken two or more years of Spanish in high school.

Placement (SAS Policy in Global Languages)

Exceptions to this policy:

  • Less than two years can take 101 and 102
  • Transfer students will start at the next level
  • Students that have received credit for AP Spanish and whose results are listed in DN
  • No native students should be registered in language classes (send them to me or to Sara)

Placement (SAS Policy in Global Languages)

  • No student in SAS will receive credit for courses under placement.

  • They will receive an “E” credit

  • If you have a student that does not want to leave the course after being told he would not receive credit, send him/her and e-mail explaining that s/he will not receive credit and COPY ME, particularly if the student is from SAS

Placement (SAS Policy in Global Languages)

  • Placement exam closes MONDAY, September 8th at 8 AM. It reopens October 6th, 2025

  • Where can students find their placement results? Go to MY COURSE LIST tab in Degree Navigator.

  • PLACEMENT results are listed at the bottom of the page

Heritage/non-heritage students

Heritage/non-heritage students

  • If you think you have a heritage student in a non-heritage class, contact Sara immediately
    • Non heritage sequence: 101, 102 (or 121), 131, 132, 203, 204
    • Heritage sequence: 139, 201, 202.

Heritage/non-heritage students

  • If a heritage student is struggling in 139, let Sara know immediately.
  • The student may need to go to 121 and then take 139
  • Heritage and non-heritage students may take 203/204 OR 201/202 during the same semester.
  • They will be able to register on their own for 201/203 but will need an override for 202/204
  • 201 is not offered for the Fall 2024 semester, therefore Heritage students can register for 203 equivalent

Academics

How to be flexible and maintain accountability

  • If a student cannot maintain pace of the course, it is best that s/he drops the course (see next slide for important Fall 2025 Dates)
  • Be sure to file a warning grades when appropriate (you will receive a message from Registrars during week 8)
  • Office hours: on campus or video conference at least two hours per class per week
  • Accommodations for disabilities
  • Online policy for deadlines
  • NO instructor should move the deadline to the reading day to accommodate students. - Instead, the student will receive a new deadline during exam week in consultation with Sara
  • “T” grades are for students that are missing specific components of the course, not for a student that is missing over 75% of the course requirements
